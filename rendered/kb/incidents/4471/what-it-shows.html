How to tell them apart

  • Parser. Day and month swap: “Booked for 2 March. The invite says 3 February.” Any channel at a day-first tenant, except the booking-v3 form.
  • Form hint. Also a swap, but only through the booking-v3 form (Alder Physio, Copper Studio, Pale Harbour). The form tells people to type month first and then reads the date day first. It needs a form fix, not a parser fix.
  • One-day move. No swap: the date is one day off. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” The desk had already marked these (desk-notes.json) and, as the desk does, they stay counted under the parser where they were filed. They are footnoted in the table and have their own ticket, because the fix is different.

How the counts are made

  • One id, one report: the export repeats some reports across pages.
  • A report’s problem comes from its tenant (locale and booking form, causes.ts) and, for the one-day moves, from the desk’s own notes. It does not come from the intake tag: intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and it disagrees with the tenant in both directions.
  • The table is computed at render time from reports-4471.json (problems.ts). No number on this page is typed by hand.

Lessons

  • A big ticket is not always one bug. Read a sample of bodies before you mark it known.
  • Count by what the report shows, not by its subject or its intake tag.
